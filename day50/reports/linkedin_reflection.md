# Day 50 LinkedIn Reflection

🚀 Day 50/60 — Optimizing the Core ML Models of My Capstone

Today I moved from simply having a working baseline to systematically evaluating and optimizing the Machine Learning layer of my Customer Intelligence Platform.

### What I worked on

🔹 Evaluated the Day 49 baseline  
🔹 Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting  
🔹 Applied GridSearchCV for hyperparameter tuning  
🔹 Used stratified cross-validation  
🔹 Compared Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 and ROC-AUC  
🔹 Analyzed train/test performance gaps  
🔹 Investigated overfitting and underfitting behavior  
🔹 Visualized model performance and generalization  
🔹 Documented engineering tradeoffs  
🔹 Saved the optimized model pipeline

A major takeaway from today:

**Model optimization isn't simply about maximizing one metric.**

A production model must balance predictive quality, generalization, interpretability, computational cost and business requirements.

The Day 50 workflow now gives my capstone a repeatable process for comparing models instead of selecting an algorithm based on intuition alone.

Next, I want to take these optimized models toward explainability, business-driven thresholding, monitoring and deployment.
