# Advanced Customer Insights – Day 31

## Objective

Transform machine-learning customer clusters into meaningful business personas and develop strategies for each customer segment.

## Dataset Overview

- Total customers: 300
- Number of clusters: 4

## Customer Personas

### Engaged Growth Customers

- Customers: 65
- Customer share: 21.67%
- Average spending: 59.40
- Average purchase frequency: 9.25
- Average recency: 34.93
- **Strategy:** Convert engagement into higher spending.
- **Actions:** Cross-selling, upselling, product bundles, loyalty programs and personalized recommendations.
- **Business Objective:** Increase customer value.

### High-Value Loyalists

- Customers: 83
- Customer share: 27.67%
- Average spending: 79.85
- Average purchase frequency: 13.77
- Average recency: 14.54
- **Strategy:** Focus on retention and loyalty.
- **Actions:** VIP rewards, early product access, personalized recommendations, premium memberships and referral incentives.
- **Business Objective:** Increase customer lifetime value.

### Low-Engagement Customers

- Customers: 75
- Customer share: 25.0%
- Average spending: 31.88
- Average purchase frequency: 6.43
- Average recency: 73.65
- **Strategy:** Reactivate inactive customers.
- **Actions:** Targeted discounts, reminder campaigns, personalized offers and email/push campaigns.
- **Business Objective:** Increase engagement and purchase frequency.

### Potential Growth Customers

- Customers: 77
- Customer share: 25.67%
- Average spending: 53.94
- Average purchase frequency: 8.90
- Average recency: 38.82
- **Strategy:** Nurture customers toward higher-value behavior.
- **Actions:** Product recommendations, bundles, loyalty rewards and targeted promotions.
- **Business Objective:** Move customers into higher-value segments.

The clustering analysis was transformed into actionable customer personas. Each persona represents a distinct behavioral pattern and can be targeted with a different business strategy.
