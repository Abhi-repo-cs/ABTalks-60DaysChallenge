# LinkedIn Reflection — Day 56

🚀 **Day 56/60 — Capstone Mid-Review**

Today I shifted from building new features to reviewing the Customer Intelligence Platform as an actual product.

For Day 56 of my 60 Days Data Science Challenge, I reviewed:

🔹 Data preprocessing and pipeline
🔹 Churn prediction
🔹 Explainable AI
🔹 Customer dashboard
🔹 Real-time analytics
🔹 Business storytelling
🔹 Scalability and reliability

The biggest lesson was that a capstone is not finished simply because individual modules work.

The next challenge is **integration**.

An end-to-end data product needs consistent data contracts, validated inputs, understandable model outputs, automated testing, reliable deployment, and a clear user experience.

I also documented the remaining gaps and created the final roadmap:

📌 Day 57 — Integration & Validation
📌 Day 58 — Deployment & Observability
📌 Day 59 — Final UX, Testing & Documentation
📌 Day 60 — Launch & Final Presentation

The project is now moving from **feature development → final integration → launch preparation.**
