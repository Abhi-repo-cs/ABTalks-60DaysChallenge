DAY 47/60 — Capstone Project Kickoff 🚀

Today marks the beginning of the capstone phase of my 60 Days Data Science Challenge.

I am starting an end-to-end Customer Intelligence Platform called “CustomerIQ”.

The goal is to move beyond isolated ML models and build a complete business-oriented data science system.

The platform will bring together:

• Customer analytics
• RFM-based customer segmentation
• Churn prediction
• Customer value analysis
• Revenue/demand forecasting
• Interactive business dashboard
• Action-oriented recommendations

For the capstone, I have completed the initial business problem definition, dataset selection, system architecture, module planning, and Day 47–60 roadmap.

The key lesson from Day 47:

Data Science is not just about training a model. A strong real-world project starts by understanding the business problem, defining measurable objectives, designing the system, and then choosing the right analytical methods.

Next: data acquisition, exploration, cleaning, and feature engineering.
