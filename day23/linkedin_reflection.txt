Day 23 of my Data Science journey 🚀

Today I focused on Feature Selection — finding the signals that actually matter.

What I worked on:
• Analyzed feature correlations
• Calculated Random Forest feature importance
• Removed highly correlated/redundant features
• Selected the strongest predictive features
• Compared model performance before vs. after selection
• Created feature-importance and performance visualizations

The biggest lesson: more features don't automatically mean a better ML model.

Good feature selection can reduce complexity, improve interpretability, and help create a cleaner model while maintaining predictive performance.
