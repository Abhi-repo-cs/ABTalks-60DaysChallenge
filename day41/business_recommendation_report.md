# Business Recommendation Report

## Problem
Customer churn is costly because businesses often identify at-risk customers only after
engagement has already declined.

## Proposed Solution
Use a predictive risk engine that combines:
- Customer behavior
- Engagement
- Product usage
- Support activity
- Payment signals
- Satisfaction
- Customer segmentation

Each customer receives:
1. Churn probability
2. Risk band
3. Risk rank
4. Recommended retention action

## Retention Playbook

| Risk | Action |
|---|---|
| High | Customer-success outreach, payment recovery, re-engagement, service recovery |
| Medium | Targeted engagement, feature education, personalized messaging |
| Low | Loyalty/value messaging and ongoing engagement |

## Success Metrics
- Churn rate reduction
- Retention rate
- Revenue retained
- Customer lifetime value
- Intervention conversion rate
- Cost per saved customer
- Retention campaign ROI

## Experiment
A/B test targeted interventions against the current retention process. Measure incremental
retention rather than simply comparing raw churn rates.

## Caveat
A predicted probability is a risk estimate, not a certainty. Thresholds and actions
should be validated using historical data, intervention capacity, and experiment results.
