# Day 48 LinkedIn Reflection

🚀 Day 48/60 — Building the Data Preprocessing Pipeline

Today I worked on the preprocessing layer of my Customer Intelligence Platform capstone.

The focus was on turning raw customer data into reliable, machine-learning-ready data through a reproducible workflow.

- Data quality validation
- Missing-value handling
- Duplicate removal
- Feature engineering
- Categorical encoding
- Numerical scaling
- Train/test separation
- Reusable Scikit-learn pipelines
- Pipeline architecture documentation

A key lesson was that preprocessing should not be a one-time cleaning script. A production analytics system needs transformations that are consistent, reproducible, reusable, and resistant to data leakage.
