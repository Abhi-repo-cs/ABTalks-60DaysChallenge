# Day 54 — Customer Intelligence Platform
## Executive Business Insight Presentation

### Slide 1 — Executive Overview
**Objective:** Turn customer analytics into decisions.
- Customer behavior can be converted into measurable risk signals.
- Churn predictions become more useful when paired with explanations and actions.
- Operating loop: **Detect → Explain → Act → Measure**

### Slide 2 — Business Question
**How can customer analytics help teams identify risk earlier and prioritize interventions?**
- Customer retention
- Risk prioritization
- Engagement opportunities
- Decision support

### Slide 3 — Customer Health Snapshot
Track:
- Active customer base
- Average spend
- Order frequency
- Tenure
- Satisfaction
- Complaint/support activity

**Takeaway:** Customer health is multidimensional.

### Slide 4 — Churn Risk
The platform provides:
- Churn probability
- Low / Medium / High risk band
- Prediction confidence
- Key decision signals

**Use:** Prioritize customer-success capacity toward customers showing multiple risk signals.

### Slide 5 — Risk Drivers
Key workflow signals include:
- Days since last order
- Contract type
- Satisfaction
- Complaints
- Order frequency
- Spend and tenure

**Caution:** Model feature importance indicates association with predictions; it does not prove causation.

### Slide 6 — Customer Opportunities
- High-value customers showing rising risk
- New customers with weak early engagement
- Customers with repeated complaints
- Customers with declining purchase frequency
- Healthy customers for expansion/cross-sell opportunities

### Slide 7 — Recommended Actions
1. Early intervention when risk crosses an agreed threshold.
2. Match retention playbooks to dominant risk signals.
3. Escalate repeated service issues.
4. Re-engage customers with declining recency/frequency.
5. Measure intervention outcomes.

### Slide 8 — Decision Framework
| Signal | Possible action | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| High churn probability | Retention outreach | Retention rate |
| Long inactivity | Re-engagement | Reactivation rate |
| Low satisfaction | Service recovery | CSAT |
| Repeated complaints | Priority support | Resolution time |
| Strong engagement | Expansion | Revenue/customer |

### Slide 9 — Measurement
Track:
- Churn and retention rate
- Reactivation rate
- Customer lifetime value
- Revenue/customer
- Intervention response rate
- False-positive intervention rate

Evaluate model performance and business impact separately.

### Slide 10 — Executive Recommendation
Build a continuous loop:

**Monitor → Predict → Explain → Prioritize → Intervene → Measure → Learn**

### Slide 11 — Next Steps
- Connect real-time UI to model API
- Validate risk thresholds
- Run controlled intervention experiments
- Monitor model drift
- Track intervention outcomes
- Refresh executive dashboards

### Slide 12 — Closing
**From prediction to action.**
The value of customer intelligence comes from turning technical signals into clear decisions, measurable interventions and continuous learning.
