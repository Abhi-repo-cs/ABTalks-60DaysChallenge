# 📊 Day 34 — Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Customer Analytics & Churn Intelligence Dashboard

A business intelligence dashboard built with **Python, Pandas, Plotly, and Streamlit** to help business leaders understand customer behavior, churn patterns, customer segmentation, revenue exposure, and retention opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of Day 34 is to transform customer data into an **interactive, executive-friendly business dashboard**.

The dashboard focuses on answering important business questions:

* How many customers do we have?
* What percentage of customers are churning?
* Which customer segments have the highest churn?
* Which subscription plans have higher churn?
* How much monthly revenue is associated with churned customers?
* How many customers are classified as high risk?
* How are customer volume and churn changing over time?
* What business actions should be prioritized?

---

## 💼 Real-World Business Impact

Customer churn directly affects recurring revenue and business growth.

Instead of looking at raw customer records, this dashboard converts customer data into actionable intelligence that can help leadership teams:

* Monitor customer health
* Identify high-risk customers
* Detect problematic customer segments
* Understand revenue exposure
* Track churn trends
* Prioritize retention campaigns
* Make data-driven business decisions

The dashboard follows the principle:

> **Data → Visualization → Insight → Business Action**

The project uses `customer_data.csv`.

# 📈 Dashboard Features

## 1. Executive KPI Cards

The dashboard provides four primary KPIs:

### Total Customers

Shows the number of customers matching the selected filters.

### Churn Rate

Calculates the percentage of customers who have churned.

```text
Churn Rate =
Churned Customers / Total Customers × 100
```

### Revenue at Risk

Shows the monthly revenue associated with churned customers.

### High-Risk Customers

Shows the number of customers classified as having high churn risk.

---

## 2. Customer Segmentation

The dashboard analyzes customers across different segments.

The segmentation visualization helps identify:

* Largest customer groups
* Customer concentration
* Revenue contribution by segment

This allows decision-makers to understand where the customer base is concentrated.

---

## 3. Churn Analysis

The dashboard calculates churn rates across different dimensions.

### Churn by Segment

Identifies which customer segment has the highest churn rate.

### Churn by Subscription Plan

Compares churn across Basic, Pro, Premium, and Enterprise plans.

This can help identify whether specific customer groups or pricing plans require additional investigation.

---

## 4. Customer Risk Distribution

Customers are categorized into risk levels:

```text
Low
Medium
High
```

The risk distribution provides a quick overview of the current retention risk within the customer base.

---

## 5. Customer Trends

The dashboard provides time-based analysis of customer behavior.

### Monthly Customer Volume

Shows how the number of customer records changes over time.

### Monthly Churn Rate

Tracks the percentage of churned customers by month.

Trend analysis can help identify whether customer retention is improving or deteriorating.

---

# 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

* Customer Segment
* Region
* Subscription Plan
* Churn Status

When filters are changed, the KPIs and visualizations update automatically.

This allows business users to investigate specific customer groups without modifying the underlying dataset.

---

# 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard automatically identifies important business insights, including:

### Highest Churn Segment

The segment with the highest calculated churn rate is highlighted.

### Revenue Exposure

The dashboard calculates the monthly revenue associated with churned customers.

### Largest Revenue Segment

The segment generating the highest monthly revenue is identified.

### Retention Priority

If churn becomes sufficiently high, the dashboard recommends prioritizing customer retention.

---

# 🎯 Recommended Business Actions

Based on the dashboard analysis, businesses can consider:

1. Prioritizing retention campaigns for high-risk customers.
2. Investigating segments with unusually high churn.
3. Monitoring revenue at risk rather than customer count alone.
4. Creating personalized retention strategies by segment.
5. Tracking churn trends regularly.
6. Investigating subscription plans associated with higher churn.
7. Focusing retention resources on customers with significant revenue contribution.

---

# 🧠 Dashboard Storytelling Decisions

The dashboard was designed using an **executive-first storytelling approach**.

### Step 1 — Establish the Situation

The KPI cards immediately communicate the overall customer and churn situation.

### Step 2 — Identify Where the Problem Exists

Segmentation and churn charts identify which customer groups contribute most to the problem.

### Step 3 — Quantify the Financial Impact

Revenue-at-risk analysis connects customer churn to financial consequences.

### Step 4 — Understand the Trend

Time-series charts show how customer volume and churn change over time.

### Step 5 — Recommend Action

The business insights and recommendations translate analytical findings into potential business actions.

This structure prevents the dashboard from becoming simply a collection of charts.
