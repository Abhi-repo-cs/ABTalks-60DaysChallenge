Day 32 of my Data Analytics journey — Recommendation Systems 🚀

Today I built a similarity-based recommendation engine that personalizes product suggestions using customer behavior.

🔹 Calculated customer similarity using Cosine Similarity
🔹 Identified customers with similar behavioral patterns
🔹 Generated similarity-weighted Top-N product recommendations
🔹 Filtered out products already seen by the customer
🔹 Evaluated the recommender using Precision@5, Recall@5 and Hit Rate@5
🔹 Created visualizations to understand similarity and recommendation scores

The key takeaway: recommendation systems can transform customer behavior into personalized experiences by finding meaningful similarities between users.

This project helped me understand the foundations behind recommendation engines used by platforms such as Amazon, Netflix and Spotify.
