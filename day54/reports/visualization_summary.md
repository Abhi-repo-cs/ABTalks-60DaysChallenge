# Visualization Summary — Day 54

## 1. Executive KPI Cards
Customer count, average value, at-risk count and retention/churn trend.

## 2. Risk Distribution
Stacked bar/donut for Low, Medium and High risk.

## 3. Risk Driver Chart
Horizontal bars showing major model decision signals.

## 4. Customer Value vs Risk
Scatter plot:
- X = customer value/spend
- Y = churn probability
- Size = tenure/orders

## 5. Action Matrix
2×2 matrix:
- High value / High risk → retention priority
- High value / Low risk → protect and expand
- Low value / High risk → targeted low-cost intervention
- Low value / Low risk → normal monitoring

## Design principles
One business question per chart, insight-oriented titles, clear units/time periods, consistent risk terminology, and no causal claims from model importance.
